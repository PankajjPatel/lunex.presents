Service and Payment Policy

Standard 40% — 40% — 20% Milestone Framework & Scope Governance

1. The 40 / 40 / 20 Payment Structure

All standard website development and custom digital engineering projects follow our structured milestone roadmap:

• 40% Advance Deposit: Invoiced before project kickoff to initiate architectural planning, wireframes, and design drafting.

• 40% Milestone Payment: Invoiced after the functional staging preview/demo is presented and approved by the client.

• 20% Final Payment: Invoiced before final production handover, live domain connection, and credential transfer.

2. Quotations & Scope Governance

Starting prices displayed on the website are baseline indicative ranges. Official project work commences only against an agreed quotation specifying exact page counts, deliverables, integrations, and milestones.

Any subsequent feature requests or scope modifications requested during development will be documented as an addendum with an associated quotation and timeline adjustment.

3. Invoicing & Transaction Verification

We issue written digital invoices/receipts for all received milestone payments. Bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS), UPI, and verified corporate payment gateways are accepted. We never acknowledge payment completion without verified banking transaction settlement.

4. Separate Third-Party Costs

Unless explicitly bundled in the approved quotation: domain registration fees, cloud hosting server subscriptions, third-party SMS/WhatsApp API charges, and payment gateway transaction fees are directly payable by the client to their respective providers.
