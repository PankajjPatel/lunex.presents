Refund & Cancellation Policy

Milestone-Based Security & Transparent Cancellation Rules

1. Milestone-Based Protection

Because custom software and digital design involve irreversible allocation of human engineering hours, Lunex Presents operates on a phased milestone payment framework (40% / 40% / 20%) rather than demanding 100% upfront payment. This protects both client investment and agency time.

2. Advance Deposit (40%)

The initial 40% advance covers foundational discovery, design wireframes, and project scoping. If a client cancels the project before any design drafts or architecture documents are delivered, a refund minus administrative expenses (10%) may be issued. Once custom drafts or wireframes have been created and submitted, the advance deposit is non-refundable.

3. Milestone Payment (40%) & Final Payment (20%)

The second 40% payment is triggered only after the interactive staging preview/demo is presented and approved. Once approved and paid, this payment is non-refundable. The final 20% payment is due before final handover and production deployment.

4. Cancellation Process

To initiate a project cancellation, written notice must be submitted via email to lunex.presents@gmail.com. Upon cancellation, all completed assets up to the approved milestone will be handed over to the client.
