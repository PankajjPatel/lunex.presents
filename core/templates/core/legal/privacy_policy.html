Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2024 • Compliant with Information Technology Act, 2000 & DPDP Act, 2023

1. Information We Collect

Lunex Presents collects personal data that you voluntarily provide when submitting an enquiry, requesting a custom quote, requesting a design demo, or leaving a review. This may include your full name, email address, WhatsApp/phone number, business name, and project specifications.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

Your personal information is processed strictly for:

  • Responding to your project enquiries and delivering requested design proposals.
  • Preparing milestones, statements of work, and project quotations.
  • Communicating milestone progress, staging previews, and deliverables.
  • Operating our automated privacy compliance scanning tool.

3. Data Retention & Security Safeguards

In adherence to the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and reasonable security practices under Section 43A of the IT Act, 2000, we implement strict technical and administrative safeguards. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to external advertisers.

4. Third-Party Services

Where client hosting, domain registration, or cloud services (e.g. Vercel, Supabase, PostgreSQL providers) are deployed on your behalf, data handling is governed by their respective privacy terms.
